# Professional Experience

## Data Analyst Intern
**Resource Control Center (RCC) — Madinah Health Cluster**  
Madinah, Saudi Arabia | April – October 2026

### Overview

Completed a six-month internship at the Resource Control Center (RCC), Madinah Health Cluster, gaining practical experience in healthcare data analysis, business intelligence, operational reporting, and performance monitoring. Contributed to developing Power BI dashboards, preparing management reports, maintaining operational data, and coordinating task distribution and follow-up within the team.

### Key Responsibilities & Projects

#### 1. Pharmaceutical Inventory Dashboard
- Developed a Power BI dashboard to monitor pharmaceutical inventory and support daily stock monitoring.
- Updated and maintained dashboard data to support regular operational follow-up.
- Used Power Query and DAX to support data preparation and dashboard functionality.

#### 2. Operating Room Performance Dashboard
- Developed a Power BI dashboard to monitor operating room closures, including closure reasons and duration.
- Tracked canceled and completed surgeries, as well as referrals.
- Organized operational indicators into a centralized view to facilitate monitoring and reporting.

#### 3. Hajj 1447 AH Performance Analysis
- Analyzed Hajj-season performance indicators and presented the findings using Power BI.
- Prepared PowerPoint reports to communicate performance information and support management review.

#### 4. Emergency, Inpatient & Bed Capacity Dashboards
- Updated existing dashboards covering emergency services, inpatient admissions, and bed capacity.
- Retrieved and prepared data from authorized healthcare information systems to support operational reporting.
- Prepared PowerPoint reports related to emergency department operations.
